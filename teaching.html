University courses

Faculty of Information Technology, CTU in Prague — 2022 to 2025.

  • Computer Networks

    Common techniques, protocols and algorithms for communication in computer networks, focused on layers 2 to 4 of the ISO/OSI model, with an introduction to communication media, security and network administration.

    BI-PSIBachelor's

  • Network Technologies

    Technologies, components and interfaces of contemporary networks at the physical layer and its overlap with the link layer: local and long-range optical networks, Ethernet and modern wireless networks, with a focus on high-speed links.

    BI-TPSBachelor's

  • Network Traffic Monitoring

    Principles, methods and tools for monitoring network traffic — capture, analysis and visualisation of traffic data for performance evaluation, troubleshooting and security.
